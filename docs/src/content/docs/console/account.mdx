[Account](https://noket.dev/account) holds the details of your Noket account.

## Email and verification

Your email address identifies the account and receives invitations. Changing it requires verifying the
new address. If you had pending invitations sent to the old address, confirm them again after the
change.

Verification confirms that the address belongs to you. It is not an identity check.

## Sessions

The account page lists the sessions signed in to the console: the current device, the last sign-in,
and actions to sign out the current device or all devices.

Signing out of every device ends console sessions immediately. It does not delete API keys, and it does
not remove the credentials on a machine where you ran `noket login` — use
[`noket logout`](/connector/login/#log-out) there, and delete keys on
[API keys](/console/api-keys/) if they are compromised.

## Data and privacy

Requests made through Noket pass through Noket and are forwarded to the upstream that serves the model.
Request bodies are not persisted by default, and logs do not contain secrets. See
[Security and privacy](/concepts/security-and-privacy/) for the full picture, including what stays on
your machine.
