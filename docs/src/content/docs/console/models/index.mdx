[Models](https://noket.dev/models) is the list of everything you can call: models you created and
models other people shared with you. Each card shows the model's name and slug, its state, its price,
and — for models you own — its limits.

## Filters and sorting

- **Source** narrows the list to *All models*, *Created by me*, or *Shared with me*.
- **Search** matches the model name.
- **Status** narrows to models that are callable or not callable.
- **Sort** is *Custom order* by default, *Name A–Z*, or *Name Z–A*. Custom order is saved per account,
  so the arrangement you drag into place is the one you see on another device.

## Cards

A card you own shows its source: an external API base URL, or the tunnel node it is bound to. The
**Test connection** and **Members** buttons sit on the card; edit, pause or resume, and delete are
under the more menu.

A shared model is marked **Shared** and shows only its name, slug, state, and price. Its more menu has
one action: leave the model, which ends your access and removes the card from your list.

## Limits on a card

Cards you own show three limit rows — budget, tokens, and requests — with the used amount against
each limit and a progress bar. The bar turns yellow as usage approaches the limit and red once the
limit is reached. Hovering a periodic limit shows when the period resets. A limit you have not set
shows as unlimited.

## Model states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Callable by everyone with access. |
| Draft | Created but not activated; the owner can review it and test it, but nobody can call it. |
| Paused | New requests are refused. The owner can resume it. |

If a budget is exhausted, requests are refused until the period resets or the budget is raised; the
card shows the spent amount and the limit.
